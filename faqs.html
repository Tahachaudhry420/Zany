FAQs

How do I place an order?

Add items to your cart, go to checkout, and fill in your details. Tapping "Place Order" opens WhatsApp with your order pre-filled — we confirm payment and delivery with you directly in chat.

What payment methods do you accept?

We confirm payment (bank transfer, cash on delivery, or other methods) with you directly over WhatsApp after you place your order.

Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship exclusively to the United States and Canada.

How long does shipping take?

Orders to the United States typically arrive in 5–8 business days. Orders to Canada usually take 7–10 business days depending on customs.

Can I return or exchange an item?

All sales are final — we're unable to offer returns, exchanges, or refunds. If there's a problem with your order (damage, wrong item, etc.), message us directly on WhatsApp and we'll work with you to resolve it.

How do I know my size for a suit?

Each suit's product page lists available sizes. Message us on WhatsApp if you would like help choosing the right size.
