Our Story

Made in Pakistan, made for the world

Zany'sCollection began with a simple frustration: beautiful Pakistani craftsmanship rarely reached women outside the country in a way that felt modern. So we started sourcing directly from goldsmiths in Lahore and embroidery ateliers in Karachi, and built the presentation around them.

Every piece is finished by hand. Every suit is inspected seam by seam. Nothing ships until it meets a standard we'd wear ourselves.
